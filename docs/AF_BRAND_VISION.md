# Acceso Futbol — Brand Vision

Source of truth for brand and product direction. Product work should serve this document.

## Who we are

**Acceso Futbol** is Mexican soccer media for the binational fan—México and the United States—unfiltered, Spanish-first, vertical-native.

We are not a wire service. We are not a scoreboard skin. We are the voice you trust when Liga MX, El Tri, and the nights that matter are on.

Taglines we own:

- *Tu acceso al fútbol mexicano.*
- *Mexican soccer media. Unfiltered. On your feed.*
- *¿Vas a verlo sin contexto?*

## The ambition

[accesofutbol.com](https://www.accesofutbol.com/) becomes the place **fútbol mexicano lives**—the ritual open on jornada night, not a bounce-through to TikTok or ESPN.

Social remains the **megaphone** (reach, Creator Rewards, viral graphics).  
The site becomes the **ritual object** (identity, pulse, show, play, radio, sponsors).

Success is not “most articles.” Success is:

1. Default destination on matchnight for fans who already follow Acceso
2. Highest share rate of Liga MX graphics among independents
3. Best MX↔US watchability layer (dónde ver, Texas + México metros)
4. Sponsors buy **ritual slots** because fans live here
5. An experience fans have not been given before—Acceso in their ear and in their scroll in one room

## Who it’s for

- Liga MX club faithful and El Tri believers
- Binational audience (CDMX, Tijuana, GDL, MTY + Houston, Austin, Dallas, Chicago, LA)
- Adult fans with real spend (beer, telco, auto, betting, finance)—not a teen-only app
- Fans of Liga MX Femenil and the broader Mexican game, not only the men’s table

## Brand pillars

### 1. Voice (we are the take)

Acceso is editorial authority without newspaper cosplay. Hot takes, micro-análisis, player narratives, controversy—delivered as **Moments**, clips, and radio beats, not a 40-article CMS dump.

**Moments are the news product.** Format is modern; ambition is #1 Liga MX voice in the feed and on the site.

### 2. Show (vertical video is the moat)

The business already runs on TikTok / IG / YT / FB video. The site must not treat the show as an outbound “Seguir” button with empty embeds.

On-site Acceso video lives in the pulse: curated clips, Moment reels, proof of the brand. Distribution stays multi-platform; **consumption starts to settle on Acceso.**

### 3. Pulse (live without becoming SofaScore)

Live scores, jornada, Liguilla literacy, match chapters—fast and tappable—always framed by Acceso context. Data is the skeleton; voice is the flesh.

### 4. Gravity (identity that pays rent)

Claiming a club / El Tri rewrites what you see. No follow theater. No orphan favorites page as the product.

### 5. Play (why you stay between whistles)

Share cards, predicciones, Liguilla path, bracket craft—the creator-native loop that already wins organic reach.

### 6. Radio (signature novelty)

**Acceso Radio** — AI match narrator you pick like a favorite radio voice (*Caliente* / *Táctico* / *Puente*), ~30s delay by design. One feature inside the room, not the company. Later: always-on station.

### 7. Bridge (MX ↔ US)

Dónde ver, timezone-aware nights, Texas activations (Q2, Leagues Cup), Puente narrator energy. Binational is product, not a demographics footnote.

### 8. Stage (creators + events)

Creator collabs and on-site event coverage are brand, not only media-kit line items. The flywheel: event → Moment/clip → site pulse → share → sponsor proof.

## What we are not

- ESPN with orange paint
- SofaScore with no soul
- A Mundial archival tool as homepage after the final
- Banner wallpaper
- A league-tab switcher (Mundial / Liga / Selección as separate apps)
- Fake “EN VIVO TV” audio—we disclose delay and AI

## Experience principle — one room

Fans organize by:

1. **Who I am** (club, El Tri, rivalry)
2. **What’s happening now** (live, about to kick, just exploded)
3. **What Acceso thinks** (the take they’d open TikTok for)

Internal product modes (not necessarily consumer tab labels): pulse / gravity / play / listen. Lighting changes in one composition—not three nav systems.

First 10 seconds must answer: *who is speaking, and why tonight matters*—not only the score.

Empty state = **editorial weather** (transfer heat, Liguilla math, rival drama, Leagues Cup in Texas)—never “Sin partido programado” as the brand.

## Revenue principle — ritual inventory

Sell attention inside the habit:

- Patrocinador de jornada
- Native dónde ver
- Branded share-card frames
- Radio sting / presenting partner
- Moment / clip sponsorship
- Event activations that return fans to the same room

Aligned with `src/config/mediaKit.ts`. Consumer product must demonstrate the inventory; `/mediakit` remains B2B proof.

## North-star line

> Open Acceso → see my club’s world → watch the take → hear the call → play the pick → share the card—without leaving for ESPN, SofaScore, or TikTok.

## Principle

Social is the megaphone. The site is where fútbol mexicano lives. Acceso Radio makes the ritual audible—it does not replace the show, the news voice, or the room.
